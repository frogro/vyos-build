# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on technical work rather than individuals;
- provide enough detail for others to reproduce problems;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing secrets or personal information;
- welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publication of another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile.
